YouTube Dislike for Safari

Estimated dislikes from Return YouTube Dislike. Independent of YouTube, Google and RYD.

Download on the App Store

Setup

Enable YouTube Dislike in Safari Extensions settings and allow access to YouTube and the RYD API. Open a video in Safari. The toolbar popup shows likes and estimated dislikes. On iPhone and iPad, you can also share a YouTube link to YouTube Dislike.

Votes

Vote submission is on by default. Use the Safari extension settings to turn it off. Statistics still contact RYD. Sharing a link never submits a vote.
